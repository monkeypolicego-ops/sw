Meal Logging Flow — Adjusted

The user flow for logging a meal, logging activity, or planning ahead for a meal out. Two adjustments to the current Figma flow: the Planning screen now supports multi-select checkboxes so several food items can be logged collectively, and a new Eating Out branch lets members look up the Syn value of the most common meal at restaurants, browsed by cuisine.

Flow A — Log a meal (updated)

Members previously added food items one at a time from the Planning screen, returning to the list after each add. Checkboxes now let them tick every item in the meal and upload them collectively with a single action.

Flow C — Plan a meal out (new cuisine-based restaurant guide)

A new branch from the chooser: a Deliveroo-style horizontal rail shows food groups at a high level — Chinese, Thai, Korean, Greek, Italian, British, Caribbean and more. Tapping a cuisine filters the restaurant list, and each restaurant shows only its most common meal with the Syn value — a quick, scannable answer to "what will this cost me?" before eating out. A planned meal can then be added straight to the diary for that day.
