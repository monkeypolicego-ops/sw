Planning Screen — Multi-select

Interactive prototype of the updated Planning screen. Food items now carry checkboxes so a whole meal can be uploaded to the diary collectively, rather than one item at a time.

  • Checkbox per item — tapping anywhere on the row toggles it.
  • Select all / Clear all shortcut in the app bar.
  • Batch action bar — shows the live item count and combined Syn total, and stays disabled until at least one item is ticked.
  • Single confirmation — one "added" message for the whole batch.

Part of the adjusted meal logging flow · See also the Eating Out cuisine Syn guide.
