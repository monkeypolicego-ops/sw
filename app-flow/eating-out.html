Eating Out — Syns by Region

Interactive prototype of the new "Plan a meal out" screen. Members pick a region and see popular restaurants there, each showing only its most common meal and that meal's Syn value — a fast answer to "what will this cost me?" before booking a table.

  • Region chips — one tap filters the list; the member's home region is preselected.
  • One meal per restaurant — the most commonly ordered dish only, keeping the list scannable.
  • Colour-coded Syn badges — green ≤ 5 Syns, amber 5.5–14.5, red 15+.

Part of the adjusted meal logging flow · See also the multi-select Planning screen.

Restaurants and Syn values are illustrative placeholders for prototyping. Production data must come from the official Slimming World food database, with "most common meal" driven by order-frequency data per chain.
