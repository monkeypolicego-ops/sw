Eating Out — Syns by Cuisine

Interactive prototype of the new "Plan a meal out" screen. A Deliveroo-style horizontal category rail lets members browse by cuisine — Chinese, Thai, Korean, Greek, Italian, British, Caribbean and more. Each restaurant shows only its most common meal and that meal's Syn value — a fast answer to "what will this cost me?" before booking a table.

  • Cuisine rail — horizontally scrolling icon categories at a high level, like the Deliveroo food-group menu; one tap filters the restaurant list.
  • One meal per restaurant — the most commonly ordered dish only, keeping the list scannable.
  • Colour-coded Syn badges — green ≤ 5 Syns, amber 5.5–14.5, red 15+.

Part of the adjusted meal logging flow · See also the multi-select Planning screen.

Restaurants and Syn values are illustrative placeholders for prototyping. Production data must come from the official Slimming World food database, with "most common meal" driven by order-frequency data per chain.
